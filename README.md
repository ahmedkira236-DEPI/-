
##  Omar Haroun Dental Clinic
> Dr. Omar Haroun Dental Clinic is a dental healthcare clinic that aims to provide affordable and high-quality dental services for patients in Faisal and Haram. The project focuses on increasing brand awareness, building trust with potential patients, and attracting new customers through social media marketing and targeted advertising. It solves common patient concerns such as fear of dentists, high treatment costs, and lack of confidence in dental services by delivering educational, trust-building, and promotional content. The project will be presented as a one-month digital marketing plan including competitor analysis, buyer personas, SWOT analysis, content pillars, media planning, and social media content strategy.

## Project Objectives
Build a strong digital presence for Dr. Omar Haroun Dental Clinic in Faisal and Haram areas.  
Increase Facebook followers and improve brand awareness through educational and engaging content.
Attract new dental clients using targeted paid advertising campaigns.
Create trust between the clinic and potential patients by showing clients reviews

##  Project Scope
• Simplifying access to professional dental care in the Faisal and El-Ahram regions.    
• Delivery of comprehensive dental treatments with a focus on high-quality patient experience.   
• Creation of a professional and reassuring visual identity that reflects trust and alleviates patient anxiety.  
• Establishment of a strong presence on social media platforms (specifically Facebook) for marketing and education.  
• Utilizing digital tools for lead generation and effectively showcasing the clinic's specialized services.

## Project Plan (5 Weeks)

###  Week 1 — [ Market Analysis & Branding]
• Conduct a comprehensive SWOT analysis and audit local competitors (Dyar, M Smile, Asnani) to identify market gaps.   


### Week 2 — [Audience Profiling & Pricing]
• Define the primary Buyer Persona (Mohamed Mahmoud) and finalize the "Price Penetration" model for core services.
